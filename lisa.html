Artist Bio · BLACKPINK

Lisa — The Performer Who Refused to Sit Still

The group's main dancer turned her restlessness into three separate careers running at once — and made all three look effortless.

Buriram, Thailand — Debut: 2016

Chapter I

The Only Non-Korean Member, and the Group's Best Dancer

Lalisa Manoban moved from Thailand to Korea as a young teenager to train, becoming BLACKPINK's main dancer and rapper — and, for years, the group's most visible connection to Southeast Asia's massive K-pop fanbase.

Her dancing became something close to a genre unto itself online: full stadiums of fans learning her choreography note for note, long before she'd released a single solo song.

Chapter II

LLOUD, and Betting on Herself

Lisa founded her own label, LLOUD, and released "Rockstar" as her first single under it — a clear statement that she intended to run her solo career on her own terms rather than folding it into someone else's system.

The pace never really slowed from there: more singles, a featured verse with Doja Cat and RAYE on "Born Again," and a full solo album, "Alter Ego," arriving in early 2025.

Chapter III

From Stage to Screen

In 2025, Lisa made her acting debut in the third season of HBO's "The White Lotus" — a genuinely unexpected pivot for a performer already stretched across music, dance, and a growing business empire, and a sign of how far she's willing to push past the K-pop-idol label entirely.

Between the label, the music, and now acting, Lisa's career increasingly looks less like a solo era and more like a full media company built around one very hard-working person.
