Addison Rae — The Influencer Who Made Pop Take Her Seriously

Dismissed for years as 'just' a TikTok star, she spent that same time quietly building the pop persona critics eventually couldn't ignore.

Lafayette, Louisiana — Breakthrough: 2024

Chapter I

From TikTok's Biggest Name to a Skeptical Music Press

Addison Rae became one of TikTok's most-followed creators years before she released serious music, which meant her early singles were mostly reviewed against the assumption that they'd be a vanity side-project rather than a real artistic effort.

That assumption started cracking with "Diet Pepsi" in 2024 — a genuinely well-received, dreamy pop single that critics who'd previously dismissed her were forced to reckon with on its own terms.

Chapter II

Addison, and Owning the Scrutiny

Her 2025 debut album "Addison" leaned directly into the tension of her own fame — being endlessly watched, judged, and underestimated — rather than trying to hide from it.

2026's "Fame is a Gun" continues that same thread: an artist who's stopped trying to convince anyone she's more than an influencer, and started making the scrutiny itself the subject of the song.
