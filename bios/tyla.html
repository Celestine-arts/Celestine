Tyla — The Voice Bringing Amapiano to the World

One song turned a South African teenager into the artist introducing an entire regional sound to global pop radio.

Johannesburg, South Africa — Breakthrough: 2023

Chapter I

"Water", and a Sound Nobody Outside Africa Had Charted Before

Tyla Laura Seethal's 2023 single "Water" fused amapiano, the percussive South African house style, with radio-ready pop songwriting — and became a genuine global hit, reaching audiences that had little to no prior exposure to the genre.

In 2024, it earned her the first-ever Grammy for Best African Music Performance, a category the Recording Academy created partly in response to how undeniable Afrobeats and amapiano had become internationally.

Chapter II

Not a One-Song Story

Rather than chasing a repeat of "Water" specifically, Tyla has kept building out a fuller pop identity — "Push 2 Start," and in 2026, "She Did It Again" and "CHANEL" — songs that keep the amapiano rhythm section but stretch her range as a vocalist and performer.

At barely into her twenties, she's become one of the most visible ambassadors South African pop has ever had on the global stage — a responsibility she's said she doesn't take lightly.
