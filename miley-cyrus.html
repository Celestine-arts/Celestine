Miley Cyrus

Miley Ray Cyrus is an American singer, songwriter, and actress recognized as an influential figure in popular music for her evolving artistry and image reinventions. After establishing herself as a child actress, she emerged as a teen idol by portraying Miley Stewart in the Disney Channel series *Hannah Montana* from 2006 to 2011. This role helped her grow a profitable franchise and achieve two number-one soundtracks on the Billboard 200, successfully launching her adult entertainment career.
