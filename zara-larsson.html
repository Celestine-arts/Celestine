Zara Larsson — The Pop Star Having Her Second Wind

A decade after her first hits, she's suddenly everywhere again — not through a reinvention, but by just outlasting the trend cycle.

Solna, Sweden — Debut: 2012

Chapter I

A Child Star Who Actually Made the Jump

Zara Larsson won Sweden's "Talang" (Sweden's Got Talent) at age ten and spent her teenage years building toward an international pop career — a path that derails far more child performers than it launches.

By her early twenties, hits like "Lush Life" and "Never Forget You" had made her a genuine global pop presence, with a vocal power that stood out even in a crowded Scandi-pop scene.

Chapter II

The Feature-Artist Resurgence

Rather than fading as pop's attention moved to newer names, Larsson has spent 2025 and 2026 rebuilding momentum through a wave of high-profile features and collaborations — "Stateside," "She Did It Again," "Midnight Sun" — stacking herself back into heavy rotation one feature at a time.

It's a less flashy comeback than a reinvention or scandal-driven headline, but arguably a more durable one: proof a strong voice and steady output can out-cycle any given pop trend.
